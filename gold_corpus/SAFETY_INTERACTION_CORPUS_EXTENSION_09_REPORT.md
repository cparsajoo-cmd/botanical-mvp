# Safety & Interaction Corpus — Extension 09

## Why this extension
The uploaded repository was audited before construction. Human-efficacy,
regulatory, taxonomy, EMA/ESCOP, and WHO corpora are already present, while
claim-level Safety/Interaction coverage remains comparatively sparse.

The two dedicated safety Gold Cases for Hypericum and Ginkgo were deliberately
not duplicated here.

## Added coverage
12 claim-level safety records across 7 botanicals:
- Hedera helix: age restriction and hypersensitivity
- Echinacea purpurea: immune-system warnings and serious hypersensitivity
- Matricaria recutita: CYP450 interaction report and serious allergy
- Glycyrrhiza / liquorice: contraindications and multiple drug interactions
- Frangula bark: GI/pregnancy/paediatric contraindications and electrolyte-related interactions
- Garlic: warfarin/perioperative bleeding warning
- Black cohosh: hepatotoxicity safety signal

## Sources
Only official EMA/HMPC/EMEA and WHO-hosted material is used.

## Important distinction
Some underlying monographs were already present elsewhere in the Gold Corpus
for efficacy or authoritative-source coverage. They are reused here only for
different, explicitly located safety assertions. This is not a duplicate Gold
Case and does not create a second source of truth for the case registry.

No Production Logic, safety rule, scoring rule, regulatory logic, or Gold Case
was modified.
