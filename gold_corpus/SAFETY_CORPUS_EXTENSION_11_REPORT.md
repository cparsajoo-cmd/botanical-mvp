# Safety Corpus — Extension 11

## Purpose
Extend high-risk safety coverage without changing production rules or creating
new Gold Cases.

## Added claim types
- pregnancy/lactation contraindications
- paediatric age contraindications
- gastrointestinal contraindications
- hepatobiliary contraindications
- seizure-history contraindication
- severe allergy/cross-allergy
- long-term stimulant-laxative dependence warning
- estragole/genotoxic-carcinogen exposure constraint

## Botanicals
- Senna leaf
- Senna pod
- Cascara bark
- Peppermint oil
- Caraway fruit
- Bitter fennel fruit
- Agnus castus fruit

## Source integrity
All records use official EMA/HMPC public summaries or final EU herbal monographs.
No Production Logic, scoring, safety rules, regulatory logic, or Gold Case files
were modified.
