# Gold Corpus Recount + Extension 10

## Recount
The current corpus was recounted before creating Extension 10.

- Independent Corpus records after Extension 09: 131
- Unique human PubMed records: 82
- Active Gold Cases: 21
- Calibration V1 is excluded from the independent-record count because it aggregates 24 already-existing records.
- Minimum planning target: ~180 independent records, subject to coverage saturation.

This means the Corpus was about 73% of the minimum planning target before Extension 10.

## Why Extension 10
A standalone Dose / Preparation / Route applicability corpus did not yet exist.
The project had three PREPARATION_SPEC Gold Cases (Valerian, Ginkgo, Hypericum),
so those botanicals were deliberately excluded from this extension to avoid
duplicating their case-specific truth.
