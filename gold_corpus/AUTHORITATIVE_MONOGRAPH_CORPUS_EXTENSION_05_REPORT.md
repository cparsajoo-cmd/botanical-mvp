## Important governance decision
WHO was not expanded at item level in this pass. The WHO collection itself is
officially available, but individual plant records were not added unless an
item-level locator could be verified to the same standard used for EMA and ESCOP.
This avoids inventing or over-interpreting WHO monograph content.
