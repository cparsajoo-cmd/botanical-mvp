## Why only two records?
This extension intentionally prioritizes source integrity over record count.
Only WHO monographs whose item-level medicinal-use text and locator were
verified from WHO-hosted material in this pass were accepted.

## Records
1. Bulbus Allii Sativi — Allium sativum L. — bulb
   - WHO Monographs on Selected Medicinal Plants, Volume 1
   - Medicinal uses section, p. 20
   - Clinical-data-supported scope includes hyperlipidaemia management and
     prevention of age-dependent atherosclerotic vascular changes; mild
     hypertension is described as a possible use.

2. Flos Chamomillae — Chamomilla recutita (L.) Rauschert — dried flowering heads
   - WHO Monographs on Selected Medicinal Plants, Volume 1
   - Dosage forms and medicinal uses, pp. 88–89
   - Clinically supported scope includes digestive symptoms, restlessness/mild
     insomnia, external skin/mucosal inflammation, and inhalational relief of
     respiratory irritation associated with the common cold.
