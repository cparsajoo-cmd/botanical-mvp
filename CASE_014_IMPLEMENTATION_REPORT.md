# Case 014 — Ginkgo biloba safety interaction

## Decision

**FREEZE / APPROVE AS REFERENCE-CURATED**

## Scope

- Taxon: *Ginkgo biloba* L.
- Plant part: folium
- Domain: `SAFETY`
- Assertion: `INTERACTION`
- State: `PRESENT`
- Severity: `MODERATE`
- Subject: concomitant use with dabigatran etexilate

## Governing source

European Union herbal monograph on *Ginkgo biloba* L., folium,
EMA/HMPC/321097/2012, final, adopted 28 January 2015.

The source states in section 4.5, page 4/8: “Caution is advised if combining
G. biloba and dabigatran.” The case is intentionally limited to dabigatran
etexilate and does not generalize the warning to every anticoagulant.

## Preparation applicability

- Dry extract
- DER 35–67:1
- Acetone 60% m/m
- Oral use
- Adults and elderly

## Severity rationale

`MODERATE` is used because the monograph requires caution and identifies a
mechanism that may increase dabigatran exposure, but does not state an absolute
contraindication for this combination.
