# Botanical Identity Corpus — Extension 13

## Why this extension
Botanical Identity had 12 dedicated corpus records after Extension 08, below the
planned 15–20 range. This extension adds eight non-overlapping official Kew POWO
records and brings the dedicated Identity corpus to exactly 20 records.

## Added
Accepted:
- Silybum marianum
- Allium sativum
- Camellia sinensis
- Crataegus monogyna
- Vitex agnus-castus
- Carum carvi

Synonym resolution:
- Zingiber zingiber -> Zingiber officinale
- Crataegus monogyna var. javorkae -> Crataegus monogyna

## Decision
Botanical Identity is now considered coverage-saturated for the current Gold
Corpus phase. Do not add more Identity records merely to increase record count.

No Production Logic or Gold Case was modified.
