# Reference Adjudication — Cases 005 and 023

Date: 2026-08-08
Scope: validation/reference layer only. No production decision rule changed in this adjudication.

## Case 005 — Cimicifuga / Actaea racemosa, menopausal symptoms

Original reference state: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, based on the 2012 Cochrane review (PMID 22972105).

Adjudication evidence:
- 2012 Cochrane systematic review: insufficient evidence to support black cohosh for menopausal symptoms.
- 2023 updated pairwise meta-analysis (PMID 37192826): potentially beneficial effects for menopausal symptoms; however, its pooled intervention scope includes black cohosh alone or combined with other active ingredients, limiting direct comparability with the original monotherapy-focused case.
- 2026 systematic review (PMID 41401209): newer same-domain evidence confirms continuing variability/heterogeneity in the trial evidence base.

Adjudicated benchmark decision: EXPERT REVIEW REQUIRED.

Rationale: the current evidence base contains materially discordant same-domain systematic-review evidence and preparation/scope heterogeneity. A single unconditional INSUFFICIENT label no longer represents the evidence state adequately. The historical GoldCase source record is retained unchanged for provenance; only the validation-layer final benchmark label is superseded.

## Case 023 — Momordica charantia, fasting blood glucose

Original reference state: null/ABSENT -> INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, based on the 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis (PMID 38274207), which reported no statistically significant fasting-blood-glucose effect in the change-score meta-analysis.

Adjudication evidence:
- 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis (PMID 38274207): null fasting-blood-glucose estimate versus placebo.
- 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis (PMID 41280283): reports improved glycaemic control including fasting blood glucose.

Adjudicated benchmark decision: EXPERT REVIEW REQUIRED.

Rationale: newer same-domain high-level evidence conflicts with the frozen null evidence state. The scientifically appropriate benchmark behavior is abstention/expert review until scope, population, preparation, study inclusion, and analytic differences are reconciled. The historical GoldCase remains unchanged for provenance.
